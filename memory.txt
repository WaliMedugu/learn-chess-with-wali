Learn Chess with Wali - Feature Memory

Initial Setup:
- Created Flutter project targeting Android.
- Added packages:
  - `chess` (rules engine and move validation)
  - `flutter_svg` (for rendering crisp, lightweight vector pieces)
  - `provider` (state management)
  - `chess_vectors_flutter` (high-quality scalable SVG pieces)

Implemented Features:
1. High-Performance Chess Engine Logic:
   - Full legal move validation (castling, en passant, promotion, checkmate, stalemate, draw).
   - Play modes: VS Computer (AI) and Local Pass & Play.
   - 3 AI difficulty levels: Easy (depth 1), Medium (depth 2), Hard (depth 3) using Minimax with alpha-beta pruning, move sorting, and positional piece-square tables.
2. Premium UI/UX:
   - Modern, sleek dark mode theme with glassmorphic elements.
   - 3 Customizable board themes: Classic Emerald, Sleek Charcoal, Ocean Blue.
   - Visual move hints: highlight selected piece, show dots on empty legal destination squares, show rings on capture squares, highlight the last move played.
   - Scorecards tracking captured pieces and score advantage.
   - Move history with rollback FEN history.
   - Game controls: Undo move, Restart, Flip board.
   - Beautiful, fully integrated Settings panel (Game Mode, Difficulty, Color selection, Board Themes).
   - Pawn promotion dialog supporting selection of Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.

Dynamic Chess Coach Commentary Engine Overhaul:
- Replaced the repetitive, random dialogue selection decks with a rich, contextual, and emotionally responsive commentary engine for Prof. Solomon.
- Implemented real-time board analysis including:
  - **Material Balance Tracking**: Compares player and AI material score (P=1, N/B=3, R=5, Q=9) dynamically. If user is up (+3), Solomon reacts as "scared/nervous"; if AI is up (+3), Solomon acts as a "gentle teacher" offering encouraging positional suggestions.
  - **Opening Book Detection**: Reconstructs move sequence in UCI format to identify 8 classic openings (Sicilian, French, Caro-Kann, Scandinavian, Queen's Gambit, Ruy Lopez, Italian Game, King's Indian) exactly on the turn they are established.
  - **Attack/Defense Scanning & Hanging Pieces**: Detects if the user moves a piece to an undefended square under opponent attack and offers friendly tutorial prompts with undo suggestions.
  - **Check/Checkmate Shock**: Captures checkmate and check moves on both sides to prompt highly specific and emotional reactions (e.g. shock when Solomon's queen is captured or he gets checked/mated).
- Fixed a bug where capture and castling flags were crossed-wired, resulting in incorrect general replies.
